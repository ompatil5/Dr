NGO & Community Charity Work

Social Impact, Free Diagnostic Camps, and Philanthropic Care

Social Impact

Philanthropic Healthcare & Community Care

Dr. Firdous Shaikh believes quality metabolic healthcare should be accessible to all, regardless of socio-economic background.

Free Insulin Support Fund

Providing free monthly supplies of essential insulin vials, needles, and glucometer strips to underprivileged Type 1 diabetic children.

  • Monthly Insulin Rationing
  • Pediatric Glucometer Kits
  • Nutritional Guidance for Families

Slum Health Screening Camps

Conducting free mobile diagnostic camps across Mumbai's urban settlements, offering spot HbA1c testing and physician advice.

  • Instant Blood Sugar Checks
  • Free Eye & Foot Screenings
  • Free Essential Medicines

Women's Metabolic Outreach

Targeted screening drives for low-income women to detect Gestational Diabetes, PCOS, and thyroid dysfunctions early.

  • Antenatal Blood Sugar Screening
  • Hormonal Health Counseling
  • Free Iron & Calcium Supplements
