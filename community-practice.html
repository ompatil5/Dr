Community Practice

Local Healthcare Networks and Outpatient Consultation Programs

Primary Healthcare

Grassroots Diabetes Care & Community Practice

Delivering high-quality, compassionate metabolic care directly into urban communities and neighborhood health centers.

Primary Center Integration

Collaborating with local community dispensaries to establish standardized diabetes screening and early intervention protocols.

  • Standardized HbA1c Guidelines
  • Primary Physician Training
  • Affordability Focused Prescriptions

Diabetes Self-Management Education

Group learning sessions empowering diabetic patients and their families to understand carb counting, foot care, and hypoglycemia safety.

  • Weekly Group Workshops
  • Vernacular Educational Materials
  • Family Support Counseling

Preventive Complication Screening

Early routine screening for microvascular complications including diabetic retinopathy, nephropathy, and peripheral neuropathy.

  • Annual Fundus Eye Evaluation
  • Urine Microalbumin Testing
  • Vibration Perception Foot Checks
