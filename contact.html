Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about initial visits, blood test preparation, and reports.

Do I need to fast before my first consultation?

For initial evaluations involving Fasting Blood Sugar and lipid profiles, 8-10 hours of overnight fasting is recommended. However, HbA1c tests can be performed at any time of day.

What documents should I bring?

Please bring your previous medical history files, past HbA1c test reports, prescription history, and your current glucometer reading log if applicable.

How do online video consultations work?

Once booked, you will receive a secure video consultation link via WhatsApp/Email along with instructions to upload your latest lab reports prior to the call.

How are emergency blood sugar spikes handled?

For severe hypoglycemia (blood sugar < 70 mg/dL with confusion) or extreme spikes with vomiting, please visit the nearest hospital casualty immediately or call emergency lines.
