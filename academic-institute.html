Academic Leadership

Faculty Positions & Medical College Affiliations

Dr. Firdous Shaikh actively contributes to medical education, mentoring postgraduate fellows, and training clinicians.

Associate Faculty

Postgraduate Teaching
Department of General Medicine

Conducting clinical bedside rounds, endocrinology case discussions, and advanced diabetology seminars for DNB & MD postgraduate trainees.

Research Fellowship Supervisor

Clinical Trials
Metabolic Research Division

Guiding clinical research fellows in designing observational studies on insulin resistance, gestational diabetes, and SGLT2 inhibitor efficacy.

CME Board Director

Continuing Education
Diabetology Training Cell

Designing curriculum and modules for Continuing Medical Education (CME) credits certified by state medical councils.

Research Alliances

Institutional Research Partnerships

Collaborating with national institutes and medical boards to advance South Asian metabolic clinical trials.

ICMR COLLABORATION
South Asian Type 2 Phenotype Study

Investigating visceral adiposity and thin-fat Indian phenotype variations in early-onset Type 2 diabetes mellitus.

NATIONAL REGISTRY
Gestational Diabetes Outcome Registry

Multi-center tracking registry analyzing post-partum HbA1c normalization rates among urban Indian mothers.

PCOS RESEARCH CELL
Endocrine & Ovarian Metabolic Alliance

Joint clinical study evaluating lifestyle intervention protocols vs metformin monotherapy in adolescent PCOS.

Mentorship

Nurturing the next generation of compassionate endocrinologists and primary care diabetology specialists.

Clinical Mentorship
Bedside Teaching

Hands-on clinical training in diagnostic interpretation, insulin titration schedules, and patient communication skills.

Academic Guidance
